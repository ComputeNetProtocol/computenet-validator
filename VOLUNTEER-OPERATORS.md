## Volunteer independently administered ComputeNet operators

The public Verification Centre is live: https://www.compute-net.org/verification-centre

We invite volunteers to qualify the native ComputeNet candidate **off mainnet**, using infrastructure and keys they control independently. Native consensus, COMPUTE payments/rewards and issuance are **not active**. No independent security audit or independently administered production network is claimed. This is not an investment offer, a deposit request or a promise of returns.

- Instructions, source, checksums and limitations: https://www.compute-net.org/validator-qualification

### How to volunteer
Reply in the [official intake issue](https://github.com/ComputeNetProtocol/computenet-validator/issues/1), or link an operator-controlled public evidence page. No founder credentials or financial deposit are needed. Initially provide only:
1. A public alias and whether you independently administer your own host and keys.
2. The hardware/hosting region, resources and period you can actually commit; any real cost quote may be redacted. Do not post billing/account secrets.
3. Whether you consent to **off-mainnet** synthetic-fund qualification, history/receipt validation, WAN measurements, restart/recovery and a coordinated founder-node outage.
4. Your preferred public coordination route. Publish a numeric HTTPS qualification endpoint, public identity and pinned public certificate only once your own firewall/TLS/sandbox/recovery checks pass. Never use Gamma/Seed production as a writable test target.

We will collect actual source/bootstrap/runtime hashes, independently validated histories, resource throughput, WAN latency/loss, recovery and separate-administration evidence. A signature or several keys alone does not prove separate people or honest resources. Operator/resource acceptance is a launch-safety check, not a permanent founder-controlled protocol admission committee.

### Optional later canary participation — no payment now
A separately approved canary would debit exactly **0.00000008 COMPUTE total**: four **0.00000001** verifier payouts plus four **0.00000001** producer fees, zero new issuance. A lawful existing holder may volunteer to be the payer; four eligible verifiers may volunteer as recipients. State the role and explicit willingness to participate, and provide only your own public address when ready. Consent to participate is **not** transfer/broadcast authority, and recipient participation is not a promised income. No funds are collected through this issue; no wallet or funding account is assumed.

**Never post private keys, seed phrases, passwords, tokens, wallet files, confidential workloads or identity documents.** Volunteers keep their own credentials. No production activation, actual funds, mining, issuance or governance handover follows automatically from replying.

Historical mainnet remains protected at eight blocks, 350 issued / 342 reconciled balance. The original 21-million cap and subsidy schedule are preserved pending explicit future activation decisions. The founder is intended to become one ordinary participant; genuine independent operation must be demonstrated, not simulated.

Please use the linked official intake issue as the single public intake point. No external operator/resource commitment is asserted until a real participant responds and evidence is checked.
